
# Call Script

Example:

Hi this is Kristen with Bed Brigade.  I am calling to schedule your delivery.  Would you be available this Saturday between 10am and noon to receive your beds?  Great.  Let me verify your information.  I have your address as 123 Main Street with a zip code of 43123.  Is that correct?  Is there an apartment number with that address?  I see you requested 2 beds for a 5 year old boy and a 3 year old girl.  Is that still correct?  Please make space available for us to setup your beds.  Our team will come and assemble the beds in whatever room you would like.  Also please secure any pets.  There must be an adult present to receive the beds.  We look forward to seeing you on Saturday.  Thanks.
